# Final validation and contribution decision

The protocol was frozen before Phase 8 outcomes and after the earlier results. It is a bounded explanatory validation, not a fresh preregistration of the entire study or a rescue of the failed acquisition candidate.

The independent coupled Duffing plant changes both domain and potential family. Forty-five polynomial force fits use five fresh seeds and 512 noisy force pairs each. Global force RMS for unrestricted/odd/Hamiltonian models is 0.50148/0.48487/0.40054 under local coverage, 0.02146/0.01634/0.01755 with 10% global coverage and 0.00639/0.00511/0.00507 globally. Coverage dominates; dimensions are 20/12/14, so this is not a parameter-matched comparison. No Duffing control or RL result is claimed.

Twenty odd-force fits explicitly grant knowledge of the true symmetry center. Global force RMS falls from 0.44018 to 0.13399 after recentering; the omitted harmonic remains. This validates the incorrect-origin explanation without granting free symmetry-center discovery in a general problem.

Four unsaturated exact-force held-controller evaluations reduce momentum RMS from 0.035818 to 0.004248 as the hold interval decreases from 0.1 to 0.0125. The log-log slope is 1.025, consistent with first-order action-hold error. These controls are not the clipped Phase 7 primary experiment.

The independent gradient and augmented-QR checks pass; see `verification.json`. There are zero new SAC runs and zero acquisition variants. The Phase 7 stop decision remains intact. The final manuscript is an original empirical research study with classical analytical tools, not a novel successful algorithm paper. Absolute priority and publication acceptance remain unverified.
